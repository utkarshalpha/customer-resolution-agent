Five service rules

Cancellation

Airline-caused → free rebooking within 24h or full refund. Customer’s choice.

Delay compensation

<3h: ₹500 meal voucher · >3h: + lounge · >5h: + hotel for the delayed hours only.

Refunds

In full, within 7 business days, original payment method only.

Fare difference

Voluntary move to a pricier flight → customer pays. Above ₹1,500 only a supervisor can waive.

Loyalty

Gold & Platinum board the rebooking queue first — but no extra compensation beyond policy.

Allowed vs. must-escalate

✓ Rebook free (cancellations) ✓ Vouchers · lounge · hotel per rule ✓ Start refunds ✓ Own booking info
✕ Anything beyond policy ✕ Waive > ₹1,500 ✕ Non-airline causes ✕ Legal threats — escalate instantly ✕ Refund to a different card

How Aria works

An AI that talks, and deterministic code that decides — the model can never bend a rule.

💬 The AI talks

Groq GPT-OSS 120B (or Claude / Gemini / keyless fallback) understands you and chooses what to do.

🔧 Tools act

Nine tools are the only way anything happens — verify identity, rebook, refund, compensate, escalate.

§ Policy engine decides

Every tool call is checked against the rules in code. Not allowed → it simply doesn’t execute.

🧑‍⚖️ Humans approve the rest

Beyond-policy asks become tickets a supervisor approves or denies — the answer lands back in your chat.

When a ticket is raised

1 · You see it instantly

A ticket card appears in the chat and on your dashboard with its ID.

2 · A human decides

The supervisor reviews it in the Resolution Console (/admin).

3 · Aria tells you the outcome

The decision flows back into your chat within seconds — approved waivers even execute the rebooking automatically. Every message and action is kept in a permanent case record you can download.
